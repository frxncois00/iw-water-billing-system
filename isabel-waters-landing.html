About us

Isabel Waters handles water connections, meter readings, billing, and service requests. You can manage your account here or work with the office in person, by phone, or by email.

Frequently asked questions

How do I view my water bill?

Log in, open Billing accounts, and enter your Contract Account Number (CAN). Select that account to see its invoices, balance, and posted payments.

Can two people link the same CAN?

Yes. Each person uses their own login and can link the same CAN to view or pay its bills.

How do I apply for a new connection?

Apply online, visit the office, call, or email. Keep your application reference and tracking code to check progress and submit requested documents.

Do I need to log in to report a leak?

No. Open Submit a concern and include the location or nearest landmark. You can send the report without a CAN.
